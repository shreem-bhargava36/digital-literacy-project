# 📱 Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist

## ✅ Do’s

- Maintain a professional and clean profile  
- Use respectful and polite language in posts and comments  
- Share useful, positive, and informative content  
- Keep your account secure with a strong password  
- Think before posting anything online  

---

## ❌ Don’ts

- Do not share personal or sensitive information publicly  
- Avoid posting offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content  
- Do not spread fake news or unverified information  
- Avoid unnecessary arguments or negative comments  
- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links  

---

## 📌 Note

Always remember that your online presence reflects your personality and can impact your academic and professional opportunities. 
Use social media responsibly and wisely.
