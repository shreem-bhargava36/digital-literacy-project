# 🔐 Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## ✅ General Safety Tips

- Never share your OTP, passwords, or UPI PIN with anyone  
- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links  
- Verify the identity of callers claiming to be from banks  
- Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts  
- Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible  
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions  

---

## 💳 UPI / Financial Safety

- Remember: You do NOT need to enter a PIN to receive money  
- Always check payment requests carefully before approving  
- Use only trusted and official apps for transactions  

---

## 🚨 Reporting Cybercrime

- Report online fraud at: cybercrime.gov.in  
- Call Helpline Number: 1930 (24x7 support)
