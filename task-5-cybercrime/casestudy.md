# 📌 Cybercrime Case Study: UPI Fraud

## What is UPI/Online Fraud?
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes in India. It usually happens when scammers trick users into sharing sensitive information or approving fraudulent payment requests.

## How it happens?
In a typical case:
- A student receives a phone call or message from a fraudster pretending to be a bank official or customer support agent. 
- The scammer claims that there is an issue with the user's bank account or that they have won a reward.
- They then send a payment request link or QR code and ask the user to approve it.
- Many users mistakenly believe that entering their UPI PIN is required to receive money, but in reality, entering the PIN authorizes sending money.
- Once the user enters the PIN, the money is transferred to the scammer’s account.

## Who are Targeted?
Students and young users are often targeted because they may lack awareness about such scams. 

## Consequences:
The consequences include financial loss, stress, and difficulty in recovering the money.

This case highlights the importance of awareness and careful handling of online transactions to avoid becoming a victim of cybercrime.
